3 executive decisions that move the risk

Control failures are consolidated by root cause. Persisted decisions remain visible until resolved, even with no current failed controls. The complete 26-control evidence backlog remains below.

Decision 1
Control privileged AI access and sensitive-data exposure

5 current failed control(s) · 15 named evidence item(s)
In progress
Assigned owner (self-reported)Identity ProtectionDue date2026-08-08
Recommended ownerIAM + Data ProtectionTarget SLA7 daysScan priorityImmediate

Why now: Broad consent, privileged application permissions and missing data controls create paths from an AI service to organisation-wide information.

Observed evidence: 8 AI applications hold org-wide admin consent for sensitive data permissions. 4 AI applications can write to every file in the tenant.

Executive choice: Reduce delegated and application permissions to least privilege, narrow consent where applicable, and enforce DLP for sensitive AI interactions. Retain broad access only as a time-bound, documented exception.

5 control(s) enter one accountable campaign; each retains its canonical verification criteria; AISPM-1001, AISPM-1003, AISPM-1005, AISPM-2001, AISPM-2002.

Decision 2
Contain unattended and high-reach AI identities

4 current failed control(s) · 9 named evidence item(s)
Approved
Assigned owner (self-reported)SecOpsDue date2026-08-15
Recommended ownerIdentity Security / SecOpsTarget SLA72 hours for High; 14 days otherwiseScan priorityImmediate

Why now: Unattended identities operate without a user and high-reach applications multiply the impact of one credential or permission failure.

Observed evidence: 7 AI applications have unattended (app-only) access to the tenant. 3 high-risk AI applications each have a consent-user count of at least 250.

Executive choice: Disable unused identities, reduce app-only permissions and rotate or replace long-lived credentials. Enforce existing blocked decisions and approve only the justified remainder.

4 control(s) enter one accountable campaign; each retains its canonical verification criteria; AISPM-1002, AISPM-4003, AISPM-1004, AISPM-1008.

Decision 3
Establish ownership and govern Shadow AI adoption

Why now: Unowned or unreviewed AI cannot be accepted, remediated or retired by an accountable decision-maker.

Executive choice: Assign an accountable business owner, decide lifecycle and sanctioning status, and record a business purpose for every application and agent.
