How to read this. One dot per vendor. Left to right is how many people it reaches; bottom to top is its risk score. Bigger dots hold more permissions.

Work the top-right corner first — high risk reaching many people. A dot high on the left is serious but contained; one low on the right is widespread but currently harmless. The user axis is logarithmic, so each gridline is ten times the last: 5 users and 500 users are not a short distance apart.
